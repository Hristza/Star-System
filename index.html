🔭 Gravity Simulator — Physics & Code

📐 Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

Every body attracts every other with a force proportional to their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

F = G × m₁ × m₂ / d² G = 0.5 (scaled gravitational constant) m₁, m₂ = masses of the two bodies d² = squared distance (+80 softening factor prevents infinity at d ≈ 0)

🚀 Euler Integration (velocity + position)

// Each physics tick: ax += G × mⱼ / (d² + 80) × (dx/d) ay += G × mⱼ / (d² + 80) × (dy/d) vx += ax; vy += ay ← velocity from acceleration x += vx; y += vy ← position from velocity

⚡ Performance Optimisations

Distance cutoff Body pairs farther than 700 px are skipped (unless one is a black hole or star).

Adaptive steps When body count > 38, only 1 physics step runs per frame instead of N.

Batch particles Particles grouped by colour — 1 fillStyle change per colour bucket.

Trail ring-buffer Trail history stored in a Float32Array ring buffer — no garbage created per frame.
