🔭 Gravity Simulator — Physics & Code

📐 Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

Every body attracts every other with a force proportional to their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

F = G × m₁ × m₂ / d² G = gravitational constant (0.5 in simulator) m₁, m₂ = masses of the two bodies d² = distance squared (+ softening to avoid ∞)

🚀 Velocity & Acceleration (Euler Integration)

// Each physics step: ax += G × mⱼ / (d² + 80) × (dx/d) ← softened gravity ay += G × mⱼ / (d² + 80) × (dy/d) vx += ax; vy += ay ← velocity from acceleration x += vx; y += vy ← position from velocity

💥 Conservation of Momentum (Merge)

vx_new = (vx₁×m₁ + vx₂×m₂) / (m₁ + m₂) r_new = ∛(r₁³ + r₂³) ← volume is conserved

🎨 Visual Realism

Planet Textures 5 procedural types: oceanic, rocky, desert, ice, gas giant — generated at spawn.

Star Lighting Planets receive directional illumination from the nearest Star. Shadow gradient applied per frame.

Accretion Disk Black holes render rotating ellipse rings in orange/gold, simulating infalling matter.

Comet Tail Ion tail length scales with speed, pointing opposite to velocity vector.

⚡ Performance

O(n²) gravity All body pairs computed each step. Distance cutoff at 700px for non-massive bodies.

Batch particles Particles grouped by color — one fillStyle change per color bucket instead of per particle.

Adaptive steps When bodies > 35, simulation does 1 physics step/frame instead of N.
